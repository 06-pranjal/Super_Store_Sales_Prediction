Super Store Management System

📌 Project Description

The Super Store Management System is a software application designed to manage the daily operations of a supermarket or retail store. It helps store owners and employees maintain product details, manage inventory, track sales, and organize billing activities efficiently.

The main purpose of this project is to reduce manual work, improve accuracy, and make store management easier and faster. It provides a systematic way to store and manage product information, monitor stock availability, and maintain sales records.

🎯 Objectives

- To manage product details efficiently.
- To maintain inventory and stock records.
- To simplify sales and billing operations.
- To reduce manual errors in store management.
- To maintain organized sales and product information.
- To improve the overall efficiency of store operations.

✨ Features

- Product Management: Add, update, view, and manage product details.
- Inventory Management: Track available stock and product quantities.
- Sales Management: Maintain records of products sold.
- Billing System: Help calculate the total cost of purchased products.
- Stock Monitoring: Check product availability.
- Data Management: Keep store-related information organized.

⚙️ How It Works

1. The user opens the Super Store Management System.
2. Product details are added and maintained.
3. The system manages available stock.
4. Sales are recorded when products are purchased.
5. Billing information is calculated and maintained.
6. The user can check product details and sales records whenever required.
